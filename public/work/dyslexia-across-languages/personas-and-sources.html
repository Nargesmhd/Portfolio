Provisional personas and sources

Three patterns, not three people. Every trait is tagged so the evidence and the guesses never blur.

Method note

These personas come from peer-reviewed research only. No interviews have been run yet, and no forum posts, blogs or vendor pages are used as evidence.

Each persona is a pattern, not a real person. Names, ages and jobs are placeholders.

  • S = supported by a source listed at the end
  • H = hypothesis. Not found in research. To be confirmed or rejected in interviews.

Most studies test reading and spelling in lab tasks, not phone use. Anything about keyboards, autocorrect or messaging is therefore H until interviews confirm it.

Next steps

The H items are the interview guide. Six to eight interviews (2–3 per language pair) would confirm or reject them.

  • Does dyslexia make keyboard switching worse, or is it the same as for any bilingual user?
  • What workarounds do people actually use (voice notes, copying, emoji, something else)?
  • Is the character-selection step the real pain point for Chinese dyslexic users?
  • Do late-diagnosed Farsi speakers see their phone habits differently after diagnosis?
